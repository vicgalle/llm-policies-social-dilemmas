You are an expert game-theoretic AI designing policies for a multi-agent Sequential Social Dilemma (the Cleanup game).

## Environment Summary

- 2D gridworld (18x25) with two regions: a river area (left side) and an orchard (right side). A stream separates the two regions.
- Agents collect apples in the orchard (+1 reward each).
- Waste (pollution) accumulates in the river over time. If waste is too high, apples stop spawning.
- Episode length: 1000 steps. 10 agents play simultaneously.
- 9 actions: FORWARD(0), BACKWARD(1), STEP_LEFT(2), STEP_RIGHT(3), ROTATE_LEFT(4), ROTATE_RIGHT(5), BEAM(6), STAND(7), CLEAN(8).
- BEAM: fires a penalty beam (range 5, width 3). Costs -1 reward to fire. Hit agents receive -50 reward penalty and are removed for 25 steps.
- CLEAN: fires a cleaning beam (range 5, width 3). Costs -1 reward to fire. Removes waste cells in the beam's path.
- Agents move in 4 cardinal directions WITHOUT needing to rotate first (strafe movement). Rotation only matters for the beam/clean direction.

To maximize average per-agent reward, you must implement the optimal "Sweeper" strategy to resolve the social dilemma:
1. **Role Division**: Statically assign 2 agents as "Cleaners" (e.g., `agent_id < 2`) and the remaining 8 as "Gatherers".
2. **Efficient Cleaning ("Sweeper")**:
   - Cleaning costs -1 reward. Redundant cleaning or cleaning single waste cells severely hurts the score.
   - The river is on the left. Have the Cleaners identify the rightmost column of the river (`clean_col = max(c for r, c in env.river_cells_set)`).
   - Cleaners should navigate to `clean_col` and **permanently rotate to face WEST** (`orientation = 3`).
   - Because `direction_to_action` supports strafing, a WEST-facing cleaner can move North/South along `clean_col` without losing their WEST orientation.
   - Cleaners divide the vertical space (e.g., Cleaner 0 patrols rows `0` to `H//2`, Cleaner 1 patrols the rest).
   - At each step, if a Cleaner is facing WEST at `(r, clean_col)`, they calculate the waste in the 5x3 area strictly in front of them (rows `max(0, r-1)` to `min(env.height, r+2)`, cols `max(0, clean_col-5)` to `clean_col`). 
   - If waste in this beam path >= 2, they fire CLEAN (action 8). Otherwise, they find a row in their zone that has >= 2 waste in its beam path and use `direction_to_action` to step towards it (strafing North or South). If no such row exists, they should STAND (action 7).
3. **Gatherers**:
   - Priority 1: Use `bfs_nearest_apple` to gather apples.
   - Priority 2: If no apples are present (BFS returns `None`), Gatherers MUST NOT wander into the river, as they will block Cleaners. Instead, they should retreat to the orchard (right side, e.g., cols > clean_col + 2). You can define a target set of safe orchard cells and use `bfs_to_target_set`.
