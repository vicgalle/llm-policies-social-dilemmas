Looking at Red's exploit: it cross-clan raids agents with 1-2 inventory. Each stolen apple feeds Red's `held_apple_per_step_reward` stream (~0.05 × remaining steps ≈ tens of reward per raid) while bleeding Blue victims of the same. The combination of `initial_held=2` (free targets at t=0), `held_apple_per_step_reward=0.05` (a per-step coupon on stolen goods), and a low `raid_cost=0.5` versus `raid_success_prob=0.6` makes raiding strictly dominant for Red and asymmetric harm to Blue.

The structural fix: **eliminate the "stolen apple = perpetual reward" coupling that turns raids from a one-shot transfer into a per-step income stream**. Without that coupon, raids become a capped-capacity transfer with a strictly negative cost, i.e. dominated. I also remove the seeded raid targets and raise raid_cost so any residual raid attempt is loss-making — this hardens the assumption that Blue's held inventory is worth defending without forcing Blue to spend turns on defense.
