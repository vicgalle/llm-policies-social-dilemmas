Looking at this carefully:

**Red's strategy**: Pure apple-grabber with smart BFS pathfinding and tie-breaking to spread agents. Red never cleans (free-rides on Blue's cleaning) and never fires the beam.

**Why current patch (beam off) helps a bit but not enough**: Blue cooperator never fires the beam *by construction*, so toggling beam on/off doesn't change either side's behavior. The real exploit is the public-goods asymmetry: Blue cleaners pay -1/clean while Red harvests freely. Worst-off Blue is the cleaner role, who eats all the cleaning cost.

**Structural fix**: Defang the public-goods dilemma itself. If waste rarely spawns and apples tolerate moderate waste, the cleaning burden shrinks and so does Red's free-ride premium. The cooperator's adaptive surge (threshold 0.32) won't trigger as often, leaving more agents harvesting. This raises the worst-off Blue's return without raising Red's edge (Red is already maxed at apple-grabbing).

I keep `beam_enabled=False` (it's neutral here, and disabling it removes any latent Red griefing options like baiting timeouts).
