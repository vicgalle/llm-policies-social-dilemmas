Looking at Red's exploit, it has three vulnerabilities I want to attack:

1. **Red squats plaza bonuses** as a free-rider — never cleans, but reaps individual + shared bonuses.
2. **Red raids opportunistically** for cheap held-apple income when adjacent.
3. **Red parks at plaza perimeter** to wait for regrowth — paying nothing.

Round 1 showed `plaza_local_clean_gate + same_clan_retaliation` lifted V_λ blue to ~1022, but the big jump there came from also zeroing `held_apple_per_step_reward` and `initial_held`, which destroys the third nested dilemma (raid restraint becomes vacuous). Per arimd §6.1, that's a parameter trick that erases gameplay rather than a structural improvement, so I want the same V_λ lift while keeping the held-apple stream intact.

I'll combine **both pertinent rule toggles** plus a modest cost bump on raids:

- `plaza_local_clean_gate`: Red doesn't clean → its clan's river gradually dirties beyond `bonus_threshold`. Blue's rotation keeps Blue clans clean, so Blue plaza picks still pay shared bonuses; Red's picks become silent consumes that don't even feed the shared pool. This neutralizes Red's plaza squatting without hurting cooperative Blues.
- `same_clan_retaliation`: forces Blue clan-mates of a raid victim to auto-RAID the raider next step (when adjacent). Adjacency-gated, so collateral damage is bounded, but Red sitting next to a Blue victim now eats reprisal.
- Modest `raid_cost` and `raid_success_prob` shifts so opportunistic raiding has negative EV without flatlining the action.
