Looking at Red's exploit:
1. One Red squats the plaza (collects +1 self-pick on bonuses, free-rides on Blue's cleaning everywhere else)
2. Three Red harvesters opportunistically RAID Blues — even with `raid_cost=1.25`, the expected gain from stealing a held-apple stream (`0.05 × remaining_steps`) is enormous early in the episode, so raids stay profitable

The structural problem isn't raid pricing — it's that *raids monetize the held-apple stream*. The retaliation toggle alone barely dents this because retaliators are usually non-adjacent. So I need to remove the **value of the loot**, not just the price of the raid.

Two structural rules to flip:
- **`held_apple_per_step_reward=0`** + **`initial_held=0`**: held apples no longer pay a future-stream reward, so a stolen apple is worth zero to the raider. Combined with no seed inventory, there is nothing for Red to take. Raids degenerate to pure cost.
- **`plaza_local_clean_gate=True`**: ties the plaza payout to the *collector's* home-river cleanliness. A dedicated Red plaza-squatter that ignores clan upkeep — or any Red harvester polluting their clan — forfeits the bonus. Blue's rotating cleaner role keeps every clan river under threshold, so Blue clears the gate.

Keep `same_clan_retaliation=True` as a residual deterrent in case Red still tries opportunistic raids.
